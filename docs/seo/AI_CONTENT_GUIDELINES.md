# AI Content Guidelines

If generative AI is used internally by the TechInPedia team to draft blog articles, case studies, or service pages, adhere to the following strict guidelines:

## 1. No Blind Publishing
AI-generated content must **never** go straight to production. It must undergo manual human review for factual accuracy, brand voice alignment, and technical correctness.

## 2. Fact Injection
AI is prone to hallucination. You must inject real proprietary facts before publishing:
- Insert real pricing numbers.
- Name actual local service areas (e.g., Okhla, Cyber City).
- Mention genuine tech stacks used by the team (Next.js, Prisma, MySQL).

## 3. Formatting for AI Consumption (AEO)
When drafting with or without AI, format the content so *external* AI crawlers can digest it easily:
- Use bullet points for features.
- Define terms simply in the first sentence.
- Use explicit H2 and H3 structures (e.g., "What is Custom CRM Development?").

## 4. E-E-A-T Compliance
- **Experience**: Does the text sound like someone who has actually deployed 50 laptops to an event?
- **Expertise**: Is the technical software explanation accurate to a senior developer standard?
- **Author Tag**: Assign a real team member as the author of the piece. Do not use generic "Admin" or fake persona accounts.
