# Brand Authority Plan (GEO/AEO)

AI models determine trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) through consensus mapping (i.e. do other trusted entities agree that TechInPedia is a software/hardware provider?).

## Legitimate External Authority Building

1. **Google Business Profile & Bing Places**: Claim and verify the physical business address (if customer-facing) or a service-area business.
2. **LinkedIn Company Page**: Maintain an active LinkedIn page. Ensure employees accurately link their profiles to the company page to establish human capital trust.
3. **GitHub Organization**: Since TechInPedia offers custom software engineering, an active GitHub organization with open-source contributions or tech documentation builds immense authority for Perplexity and developer-centric queries.
4. **Verified Case Studies**: Instead of generic testimonials, publish structured case studies and ask clients to link back or mention the collaboration on their own platforms (e.g. "Platform built by TechInPedia").
5. **Industry Directories**: Claim profiles on Clutch.co, GoodFirms, or local Indian business directories (JustDial, IndiaMart) strictly for hardware rentals.

## Forbidden Methods (Do Not Use)
- Fake reviews on Google/Trustpilot.
- Private Blog Networks (PBNs) or paid link blasts.
- AI-generated press releases.
- Fabricated awards or statistics.
